# YouTube — Complete Usage Guide

This is the reference guide Compass uses to help people learn how to use YouTube.
It covers accounts, the interface and every button, watching, subtitles,
uploading, downloads, playlists and settings — on both the website and the
mobile app.

---

## 1. Accounts — sign up, sign in, sign out

### Create an account (sign up)
YouTube accounts are Google accounts — there is no separate YouTube password.
1. Go to **youtube.com** (or open the YouTube app) and click **Sign in** (top-right on the web, top-right avatar on mobile).
2. Click **Create account** → choose **For my personal use** (or "For work/business").
3. Enter your name, then a new Gmail address (or "Use my existing email"), and a password.
4. Verify your phone number with the code Google texts you.
5. Accept the terms. You now have a Google account that signs you into YouTube automatically.

### Sign in (screen by screen)
Signing in takes you through a few Google screens. Here is exactly what each looks like:

1. **YouTube home** — When signed out, the top-right shows a **Sign in** button (person icon). The left sidebar also has a **Sign in** button under "Sign in to like videos, comment and subscribe." Click either one. If you have never watched anything, the feed just says "Try searching to get started."
2. **"Sign in — to continue to YouTube"** (Google screen) — Type your address in the **Email or phone** field, then click **Next**. On this screen you can also use **Forgot email?**, **Create account** (to sign up instead), or the **Guest mode** link ("Not your computer? Use Guest mode to sign in privately").
3. **"Enter your password"** — Google shows your name and account chip. Type your password, optionally tick **Show password** to reveal it, then click **Next**. **Try another way** lets you sign in without the password (e.g. a device prompt or backup code).
4. **2-Step Verification (if enabled)** — "Check your device" means Google sent a **Yes/No** prompt to your phone (e.g. your Honor Pad X9 / Galaxy A14) — tap **Yes** on that notification. Tick **Don't ask again on this device** to skip 2FA next time on this computer. Use **Resend it** if the prompt does not arrive, or **Try another way** for a different method (code, security key, etc.). Once approved, you land back on YouTube signed in.

Small print at the bottom of every Google screen: a language picker (e.g. **English (United Kingdom)**) on the left, and **Help**, **Privacy**, **Terms** on the right.

### Switch accounts
Click your **profile picture (avatar)** top-right → **Switch account** → pick another signed-in account, or **Add account**.

### Sign out
Click your **avatar** top-right → **Sign out**. On mobile, tap your avatar → tap your account name at the top → **Manage accounts on this device** to remove one.

### Create a channel
You need a channel to upload, comment, or make playlists.
1. Click your **avatar** → **Create a channel**.
2. Choose your channel name and picture → **Create channel**.

## 2. The interface — every button

### Top bar (header)
- **Hamburger menu (☰)** — top-left. Opens/collapses the left sidebar (Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, Library, etc.).
- **YouTube logo** — click to return to the Home feed.
- **Search bar** — type a query and press Enter or click the **magnifying glass**. The **microphone icon** lets you search by voice.
- **Create (＋ / video-camera icon)** — top-right. Opens a menu: **Upload video**, **Go live**, **Create post**, **Create Short**.
- **Notifications (bell 🔔)** — shows new uploads from channels you're subscribed to, replies, and mentions. A red dot means unread items.
- **Avatar (profile picture)** — top-right. Opens your account menu: your channel, YouTube Studio, purchases, appearance (dark/light theme), language, settings, sign out.

### Left sidebar
- **Home** — personalized recommendations.
- **Shorts** — vertical short-form videos.
- **Subscriptions** — latest videos from channels you follow.
- **You / Library** — your history, playlists, your videos, watch later, liked videos, and downloads.
- **History** — everything you've watched (can be paused or cleared).
- **Subscriptions list** — direct links to each channel you follow.
- **Explore** — Trending, Music, Gaming, News, Sports, Learning, Fashion.
- **Settings**, **Report history**, **Help**, **Send feedback** at the bottom.

## 3. Watching a video — the player and every control

Hover over the video to reveal the control bar. Left to right along the bottom:

- **Play / Pause (▶ / ⏸)** — or press the **Spacebar** / **K**.
- **Next (⏭)** — skip to the next video in the queue/playlist.
- **Volume (🔊)** — hover to reveal the slider; click to **mute/unmute** (or press **M**). Scroll the slider to adjust.
- **Timestamp / progress bar** — click or drag the red bar to jump. Hover to preview thumbnails.
- **Subtitles/CC (▭)** — turns captions on/off (see Section 4).
- **Settings (⚙ gear)** — opens a menu for:
  - **Playback speed** — 0.25× up to 2×.
  - **Quality** — Auto, or fixed resolutions (144p up to 4K/2160p, depending on the video).
  - **Subtitles/CC** — choose language and auto-translate.
  - **Sleep timer** and **Annotations** (when available).
- **Miniplayer (▭↘)** — shrinks the video to a corner so you can keep browsing.
- **Theater mode (▭)** — a wider player without going full screen (or press **T**).
- **Full screen (⛶)** — fills the screen (or press **F**; **Esc** to exit).

### Keyboard shortcuts (web)
- **Space / K** — play/pause · **J** — back 10s · **L** — forward 10s
- **← / →** — 5s back/forward · **M** — mute · **F** — full screen · **T** — theater
- **C** — toggle captions · **Shift + >** / **Shift + <** — speed up/down · **0–9** — jump to 0%–90%

### Under the video
- **Like (👍) / Dislike (👎)** — rate the video.
- **Share** — copy a link or send to apps; includes **Start at** a chosen timestamp.
- **Download** — save for offline (YouTube Premium; see Section 5).
- **Save (＋ / Save)** — add to **Watch later** or a playlist.
- **Subscribe** — follow the channel. The **bell** next to it sets notification level (All, Personalized, None).
- **Autoplay toggle** — top of the up-next list; auto-plays a related video when the current one ends.

## 4. Subtitles / captions

### Turn subtitles on or off
- **Web:** click the **Subtitles/CC (▭)** button on the player, or press **C**. When on, the button has a red underline.
- **Mobile:** tap the video once, tap the **CC** icon (top-right of the player). Or tap the **⋮ / gear** → **Captions**.

### Choose the caption language
1. Click the **gear (⚙)** → **Subtitles/CC**.
2. Pick an available language. If the creator only added one language, use **Auto-translate** → choose your language for a machine translation.

### Change how subtitles look
- **Web:** gear (⚙) → **Subtitles/CC** → **Options** — change font, size, color, background, and opacity.
- **Account-wide:** **Settings → Playback and performance** (web) or **Settings → Captions** (mobile) to set default language and whether captions are always on.

### Auto-generated captions
Many videos have machine-generated captions marked **(auto-generated)**. Accuracy varies. If a video has no CC button, no captions are available for it.

## 5. Downloads (offline) — save and delete

Downloading videos for offline viewing requires **YouTube Premium** (on mobile, and on the desktop app/PWA in supported regions). Downloads stay inside the YouTube app — they can't be exported as files.

### Download a video
- **Mobile:** open the video → tap **Download** (down-arrow icon) under the player → choose a quality if prompted. Or tap **⋮ → Download**.
- Downloaded videos appear under **You / Library → Downloads**.

### Open / watch a downloaded video
Go to **You (or Library) → Downloads** and tap the video. It plays without using data.

### Delete a downloaded video
1. Go to **You / Library → Downloads**.
2. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** next to the video (or tap the **Downloaded ✓** button on its page).
3. Tap **Delete from downloads** (or **Remove download**). This frees the storage; the video stays on YouTube to re-download later.
4. To clear everything: **Settings → Downloads → Delete all downloads**.

## 6. Uploading videos

### Upload from the website (youtube.com)
1. Click **Create (＋ camera icon)** top-right → **Upload video**.
2. Drag your video file in, or click **Select files**.
3. **Details:** add a **Title**, **Description**, and **Thumbnail** (upload a custom one or pick a frame).
4. **Audience:** state whether it's **Made for Kids** (required by law).
5. Add to a **Playlist**, set tags, language, and category (under "Show more").
6. **Video elements:** add subtitles, end screens, and cards.
7. **Checks:** YouTube scans for copyright issues.
8. **Visibility:** choose **Public**, **Unlisted** (only people with the link), **Private** (only you/invited), or **Schedule** a future time.
9. Click **Publish** (or **Save/Schedule**).

### Upload from the mobile app
1. Tap **Create (＋)** at the bottom → **Upload a video** (or record/Short).
2. Pick a clip, trim it, add a title/description, set visibility → **Upload**.

### Manage your uploads — YouTube Studio
Go to **avatar → YouTube Studio** (or studio.youtube.com) to edit titles/thumbnails, view analytics, read/reply to comments, manage subtitles, and delete videos (**Content → hover a video → ⋮ → Delete forever**).

## 7. Subscriptions & notifications
- **Subscribe:** click the red/black **Subscribe** button on any video or channel page.
- **Notification bell** (next to Subscribe): **All** (every upload), **Personalized** (some), or **None**.
- **Unsubscribe:** click **Subscribed** → **Unsubscribe**.
- Manage everything at **Subscriptions → Manage** (web).

---

## 8. Playlists
- **Create / add to a playlist:** under a video click **Save** → **＋ New playlist** (name it, set visibility) or tick an existing one.
- **Watch Later:** a built-in playlist — click **Save → Watch later**.
- **Find playlists:** **You / Library → Playlists**.
- **Remove a video from a playlist:** open the playlist → **⋮** next to the video → **Remove from [playlist]**.
- **Delete a playlist:** open it → **⋮ (or Edit) → Delete playlist**.

---

## 9. Comments, likes, and history
- **Comment:** scroll below the video, type in **Add a comment**, click **Comment**. Reply with **Reply**; like/heart others' comments.
- **Liked videos:** find them under **You / Library → Liked videos**.
- **Watch history:** **You / Library → History**. **Pause history** or **Clear all watch history** in **Settings → History & privacy** (or **Manage all history**).

---

## 10. Settings & appearance
Open via **avatar → Settings**:
- **Account** — manage channels, memberships.
- **Appearance (avatar menu)** — **Dark theme**, Light theme, or Device theme.
- **Playback and performance** — default caption/quality preferences.
- **Notifications** — control emails and push alerts.
- **Privacy** — history pausing, liked-video visibility.
- **Billing & payments** — YouTube Premium, purchases.
- **Language / Location** — bottom of the avatar menu.

---

## 11. YouTube Premium (what it unlocks)
- Ad-free videos, background play (audio keeps going when you leave the app), and **offline downloads** (Section 5). Subscribe at **avatar → Purchases and memberships** or youtube.com/premium.

---

## Quick answers
- **Turn on subtitles:** press **C** (web) or tap **CC** on the player (mobile).
- **Change quality:** gear (⚙) → **Quality**.
- **Full screen:** press **F**.
- **Delete a downloaded video:** **Library → Downloads → ⋮ → Delete from downloads**.
- **Upload:** **Create (＋) → Upload video**.
- **Sign out:** **avatar → Sign out**.
