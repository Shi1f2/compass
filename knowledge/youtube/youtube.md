every player control, subtitles and accessibility, uploading, Studio management,
downloads, playlists, comments, notifications, search, settings, YouTube Premium,
Shorts, live streaming, Kids, and common troubleshooting — on both the website (youtube.com)
and the mobile app (Android and iOS).

## 1. Accounts

### What is a YouTube account?
YouTube is owned by Google. There is no separate YouTube username and password.
Every YouTube account IS a Google account. If you already have Gmail or Google Drive,
you already have a YouTube account — just sign in with those credentials.

---

### Create a new Google / YouTube account (sign up)

**On the website:**
1. Go to **youtube.com** in your browser. In the top-right corner you will see a
   **Sign in** button (a person silhouette icon with the text "Sign in"). Click it.
2. On the Google sign-in screen that opens, click **Create account** (it appears below
   the "Email or phone" field). A dropdown asks:
   - **For my personal use** — for individual accounts.
   - **For my child** — creates a supervised account via Family Link.
   - **For work or my business** — same process but tied to a work email.
   Choose **For my personal use** if unsure.
3. Enter your **First name** and **Last name**, then click **Next**.
4. Enter your **date of birth** and **gender** (gender is private by default), click **Next**.
5. Google suggests a Gmail address based on your name. You can:
   - Pick one of the suggested addresses, or
   - Click **Create your own Gmail address** to type a custom one.
   - Click **Use your existing email** if you want to link a non-Gmail email.
   Then click **Next**.
6. Create a strong **password** — at least 8 characters, mixing letters, numbers, and symbols.
   Tick **Show password** to reveal what you are typing. Confirm it in the second box. Click **Next**.
7. Google asks you to add a **recovery phone number** (optional but strongly recommended for
   account recovery). Enter your number and click **Next**, or click **Skip**.
8. A **verification code** is sent to your phone by text. Enter it and click **Next**.
9. Review the **Privacy and Terms** page. Scroll to the bottom and click **I agree**.
10. You are now signed in. You will be taken back to YouTube automatically.

**On the mobile app:**
1. Open the YouTube app. Tap the **profile picture placeholder** (circle) in the top-right.
2. Tap **Sign in**, then **Create account → For myself**.
3. Follow the same steps as above — Google's account creation screens are identical.

---

### Sign in — screen by screen

When you are signed out, this is exactly what you see and do:

**Step 1 — YouTube home page (signed out)**
- Top-right corner: a blue **Sign in** button.
- Left sidebar: a **Sign in** panel saying "Sign in to like videos, comment, and subscribe."
- The main feed shows generic popular videos (no personalized recommendations).
- Click either **Sign in** button to begin.

**Step 2 — "Sign in to continue to YouTube" (Google screen)**
- A white card appears with the Google logo at the top and the YouTube logo.
- The heading reads **"Sign in"** with the subtitle **"to continue to YouTube"**.
- Type your **Gmail address** or **phone number** in the "Email or phone" text box.
- **Forgot email?** link — if you can't remember your address, click this to find it via
  your recovery phone number or recovery email.
- **Create account** link — if you don't have an account yet.
- **Guest mode** link (small text at the bottom) — signs you in temporarily on a shared computer
  without saving your history or preferences to any account.
- A **language picker** sits in the bottom-left (e.g. "English (United Kingdom)").
- **Help**, **Privacy**, **Terms** links sit in the bottom-right.
- Click **Next** to continue.

**Step 3 — "Enter your password"**
- Google shows your name, your profile picture (if set), and your email address as a chip
  at the top — confirming which account you are signing into.
- Type your **password** in the text field.
- Tick **Show password** (eye icon) to reveal it.
- **Forgot password?** — resets via your recovery phone/email.
- **Try another way** — if you don't have your password, Google can send a sign-in prompt
  to a trusted device, or send a code to your recovery email/phone.
- Click **Next**.

**Step 4 — 2-Step Verification (if you have it enabled)**
- Google displays "Check your [device name]" — e.g. "Check your Honor Pad X9" or
  "Check your Galaxy A14". It has sent a yes/no notification to your phone.
- On your phone, a notification appears saying "Is it you trying to sign in?" — tap **Yes**.
- If you didn't get the prompt, tap **Resend it** to try again.
- **Try another way** shows alternative methods:
  - A 6-digit code sent by SMS to your phone.
  - A backup code (if you saved them when setting up 2FA).
  - A physical security key (if registered).
  - An authenticator app code (Google Authenticator, etc.).
- Tick **Don't ask again on this device** if this is your personal computer — you won't be
  asked for 2FA again on this browser/device for ~30 days.
- After approval, you are signed in and taken back to YouTube.

---

### Switch accounts
- Click your **profile picture (avatar)** in the top-right.
- In the dropdown, click **Switch account**.
- A panel shows all Google accounts you have previously signed into on this browser.
- Click any account to switch instantly.
- Click **Add account** to sign into a new account and add it to the list.
- On mobile: tap your **avatar** → tap your **account name** at the top of the sheet →
  tap **Add another account** or tap another listed account to switch.

---

### Sign out
- **Web:** Click your **avatar** → **Sign out**.
- **Mobile:** Tap your **avatar** → tap your account name → **Manage accounts on this device**
  → tap the account → **Remove account**. This does not delete the account; it only removes
  it from that device.

---

### Create a YouTube channel
A Google account is needed to watch, but you need a **channel** to upload, comment, like, or make playlists.
1. Click your **avatar** → **Create a channel**.
2. On the "How you'll appear" screen, add a **channel name** and a **profile picture** (upload a photo
   or choose an initial-style avatar).
3. Click **Create channel**.
4. Your channel URL will be **youtube.com/@yourchannelname** (custom handle).
   You can change it later in **YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Handle**.

---

### Create a Brand Account (second channel)
A Brand Account lets you run multiple channels from one Google account — useful for a business
or a second personal channel.
1. Go to **youtube.com/account** → **Add or manage your channel(s)**.
2. Click **Create a new channel**.
3. Enter the brand/channel name → **Create**.
4. Switch between channels via **avatar → Switch account** on YouTube.

## 2. The Interface — Every Button and Panel

### Top bar (header) — web

The top bar is always visible on youtube.com.

- **Hamburger menu (☰)** — far top-left. Click to collapse or expand the left sidebar.
  When collapsed, only icons show (no labels). When expanded, full labels appear alongside icons.
  Useful to get more horizontal space when watching.

- **YouTube logo** — the red-and-white play button next to the word "YouTube". Click it at any time
  to return to the **Home** feed. On mobile, tapping it does the same.

- **Search bar** — the large white bar across the middle of the top bar.
  - Click it (or press **/** on the keyboard) to focus it.
  - As you type, a **dropdown of suggestions** appears — these are a mix of your past searches
    (marked with a clock icon) and popular searches. Click any suggestion to search immediately.
  - Press **Enter** or click the **magnifying glass (🔍)** button on the right of the bar to search.
  - Click the **X** inside the bar to clear what you typed.
  - The **microphone icon (🎤)** to the right of the search bar opens voice search:
    say your query aloud and YouTube searches automatically.
  - The **camera icon** (on some devices/browsers) opens **Search with a photo** — upload an image
    to find visually similar videos.

- **Create button (＋ / video-camera icon)** — top-right, to the left of the bell.
  Click to open a small popup menu with four options:
  - **Upload video** — takes you to YouTube Studio's upload page.
  - **Go live** — opens the live streaming setup (requires channel verification for some features).
  - **Create post** — opens the Community tab post composer (for eligible channels).
  - **Create Short** — opens the Short creation flow with camera and editing tools.

- **Notifications bell (🔔)** — top-right. The bell icon has a number badge (red circle)
  showing how many unread notifications you have.
  - Click the bell to open a notifications panel listing recent activity:
    new uploads from subscribed channels, comments on your videos, replies to your comments,
    live stream alerts, and channel memberships activity.
  - Each notification has a thumbnail, a short description, and a timestamp.
  - Click a notification to jump to that video/comment.
  - Click **See all** at the bottom to go to the full notifications page (youtube.com/feed/subscriptions).
  - The **gear icon (⚙)** inside the notifications panel links to notification settings.

- **Avatar / profile picture** — top-right, the circular profile photo (or letter if no photo set).
  Clicking it opens your **account menu** with these options:
  - Your **channel name** and email address at the top (click to go to your channel page).
  - **Your channel** — goes to your public-facing channel page.
  - **YouTube Studio** — opens youtube studio for managing your content.
  - **Switch account** — see Section 1.
  - **Sign out** — see Section 1.
  - **Purchases and memberships** — manage YouTube Premium, channel memberships, movie rentals.
  - **Your data in YouTube** — a privacy dashboard showing your activity.
  - **Appearance: Device theme / Dark / Light** — changes the color theme immediately without
    going to settings.
  - **Language** — changes YouTube's display language.
  - **Restricted Mode: Off/On** — hides potentially mature content (useful for shared devices).
  - **Location** — changes the regional content filter.
  - **Keyboard shortcuts** — opens a popup listing all keyboard shortcuts.
  - **Settings** — opens the full Settings page (see Section 15).
  - **Help** — opens the YouTube Help Center.
  - **Send feedback** — lets you report a bug or suggestion to YouTube.

- **Downloads** — offline videos (YouTube Premium only).

**Subscriptions section:**
- Lists every channel you're subscribed to with their icon and name.
- Click any channel to go directly to their page.
- If you have many subscriptions, a **Show X more** link expands the full list.

**Explore section:**
- **Trending** — what's popular right now in your country.
- **Shopping** — products featured in videos.
- **Music** — music videos and audio-focused content.
- **Movies** — free ad-supported movies and rentals.
- **Live** — currently active live streams.
- **Gaming** — gaming streams and videos.
- **News** — news channels and clips.
- **Sports** — live sports, highlights, and analysis.
- **Learning** — educational content.
- **Fashion & Beauty** — style and beauty content.

**Bottom of sidebar:**
- **Settings**
- **Report history** — content you've flagged.
- **Help**
- **Send feedback**
- **About**, **Press**, **Copyright**, **Contact us**, **Creators**, **Advertise** (small links).
- **Terms**, **Privacy**, **Policy & Safety**, **How YouTube works**.

### Channel page
When you click on a channel name or avatar anywhere on YouTube, you go to their channel page.
It has several tabs:
- **Home** — the channel's featured video or section layout.
- **Videos** — all uploaded videos, sortable by Latest, Popular, or Oldest.
- **Shorts** — only Short videos from this channel.
- **Live** — past and upcoming live streams.
- **Playlists** — playlists created by the channel.
- **Community** — posts, polls, and images the creator has shared.
- **About** — channel description, join date, total views, social links, and contact email.

The **Subscribe** button is prominently displayed. Once subscribed, it changes to **Subscribed**
with a dropdown arrow — clicking the arrow lets you change your notification preference.

## 3. Watching a Video — Player Controls and Keyboard Shortcuts

### Finding a video to watch
- **Home feed** — scroll down to browse recommendations. Each card shows a thumbnail,
  title, channel name, view count, and upload date. Hover over a card (web) to see a
  short preview clip automatically.
- **Search** — type a query, use filters (see Section 11).
- **Subscriptions** — see latest from channels you follow.
- **Trending** — see what's popular right now in your region.

---

### The video player — every control explained

When a video is playing, hover over it (web) or tap it (mobile) to reveal the **control bar**
at the bottom. The controls from left to right are:

**1. Play / Pause button (▶ / ⏸)**
- Click to toggle between playing and paused.
- Keyboard shortcut: **Space** or **K**.
- On mobile: tap anywhere on the screen to pause/play.
- Double-tapping the left side of the screen rewinds 10 seconds; double-tapping the right side
  fast-forwards 10 seconds (mobile).

**2. Previous / Next buttons (⏮ / ⏭)**
- **Previous** — goes back to the previous video in a playlist or queue.
- **Next** — skips to the next video in the playlist or the next recommended video.
- If not in a playlist, Next plays the top video from the "Up next" panel.

**3. Volume control (🔊)**
- Click the speaker icon to **mute / unmute** (keyboard: **M**).
- Hover over the speaker icon to reveal a horizontal volume slider.
- Click and drag the slider left (quieter) or right (louder).
- On some keyboards, use the media volume keys; these also work in YouTube.
- On mobile: use the physical side buttons on your phone/tablet to adjust volume while a video plays.

**4. Timestamp and progress bar**
- The **red progress bar** stretches across the bottom of the player.
- The **elapsed time** (e.g. "2:34") and **total duration** (e.g. "/ 10:45") appear to the right
  of the volume control.
- **Click anywhere on the progress bar** to jump to that moment in the video.
- **Click and drag** the red circle (scrubber) to seek precisely.
- **Hover** anywhere on the progress bar to see a small **thumbnail preview** of that moment
  and the timestamp — very useful for navigating long videos.
- **Chapter markers** — if the creator has added chapters (timestamps in the description), the
  progress bar shows white dividing lines. Hovering shows chapter names.
- Keyboard: **← arrow** jumps back 5 seconds, **→ arrow** jumps forward 5 seconds.
  **J** jumps back 10 seconds, **L** jumps forward 10 seconds.
  **0** jumps to the beginning, **1** jumps to 10% through, **2** to 20%, etc. up to **9** for 90%.

**5. Subtitles / CC button (▭ with lines)**
- Click to toggle subtitles on or off. When on, the icon shows a red underline.
- Keyboard: **C** toggles captions.
- See Section 4 for full subtitle instructions.

**6. Settings gear (⚙)**
- Click to open the player settings panel. It has three sub-menus:

  **Subtitles/CC:**
  - Lists all available caption languages for this video.
  - If only one language exists, there may be an **Auto-translate** option — choose any language
    to have YouTube machine-translate the captions in real time.
  - **Options** (appears after you choose a language) — lets you change the appearance of captions:
    font family, font color, font size (50% to 400%), background color, background opacity,
    character edge style, and window color.

  **Playback speed:**
  - Presets: **0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, Normal (1×), 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×**.
  - On many browsers you can type a custom speed by clicking **Custom** if available.
  - Keyboard: **Shift + .** (greater-than key) speeds up; **Shift + ,** (less-than key) slows down.

  **Quality:**
  - **Auto** — YouTube automatically picks the best quality based on your internet speed.
  - Manual options range from **144p** (very low data, blurry) to **2160p 4K** (very high data,
    razor sharp) — which options appear depends on what resolution the video was uploaded in.
  - **Premium Quality / 1080p Enhanced** — YouTube Premium subscribers get access to 1080p
    enhanced bitrate ("Premium" label next to 1080p).
  - On mobile: Settings → Quality → choose a resolution or leave on "Auto".

**7. Miniplayer button (▭↘)**
- Shrinks the video to a small floating window in the **bottom-right corner** of the browser tab.
- You can keep browsing YouTube (reading comments, searching) while the video plays in the corner.
- Click the **expand** icon on the miniplayer to return to full size.
- Keyboard: **I** toggles the miniplayer.

**8. Theater mode button (▭ wide)**
- Expands the video player to fill most of the browser width, pushing the sidebar away.
- The video is bigger but you are still on the same page (not full screen).
- The comments and up-next panel shift below the video.
- Keyboard: **T** toggles theater mode.

**9. Full screen button (⛶)**
- Fills your entire monitor with the video. Everything else disappears.
- Keyboard: **F** toggles full screen. **Esc** exits full screen.
- On mobile: tap the full screen icon or rotate your phone to landscape.
- Double-click the video anywhere (web) to toggle full screen.

---

### Under the video — buttons and info

**Like (👍) and Dislike (👎)**
- Click 👍 to like the video. The count increments immediately (visible).
- Click 👎 to dislike. YouTube hides the dislike count publicly, but your personal dislike
  is recorded and affects your recommendations.
- Clicking Like again removes your like (toggles off).
- You must be signed in to like or dislike.

**Share**
- Click **Share** to open a share panel with:
  - **Copy link** — copies the video URL. The URL already contains the exact video ID
    (e.g. youtube.com/watch?v=abc123).
  - **Start at [timestamp]** — tick this box and YouTube appends a time parameter to the URL
    (e.g. ?t=94 for 1:34). Very useful for sharing a specific moment.
  - **Embed** — gives you an `<iframe>` HTML code snippet to embed the video on a website.
  - **Platform icons** — WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter/X, Reddit, Email, and more depending on region.

**Download (⬇)**
- Saves the video to your device for offline watching (requires **YouTube Premium**).
- See Section 5 for full instructions.

**Save (＋ / bookmark icon)**
- Click **Save** to open a panel where you can:
  - Tick **Watch later** to save it to your Watch Later playlist.
  - Tick any of your existing playlists.
  - Click **Create new playlist** at the bottom.

**Clip (✂)**
- Available on many videos. Lets you select a 5–60 second clip and share a direct link
  to that segment. The clip is public, associated with your channel, and accessible at
  youtube.com/clip/... Others can watch just your chosen segment.

**Thanks (💲)**
- On eligible channels, you can send a small paid "Super Thanks" tip to the creator.
- Amounts vary (e.g. $2, $5, $10, $50) and the creator keeps a portion after fees.

**Subscribe / Subscribed button**
- Click **Subscribe** (red button) to follow the channel and see their uploads in your feed.
- Once subscribed, the button turns grey and says **Subscribed** with a dropdown arrow.
- Click the dropdown arrow to change your **notification level**:
  - **All** — get notified for every upload (bell icon becomes solid).
  - **Personalized** — YouTube picks which notifications to send (bell icon is an outline).
  - **None** — no notifications, but you're still subscribed.

**Channel name and avatar (below the title)**
- Click the channel's name or picture to go to their channel page.
- Shows subscriber count next to the name.

**Description (expand)**
- Click **Show more** under the title and channel info to expand the full description.
- The description often contains: chapter timestamps (clickable!), links to social media,
  affiliate links, credits, and a full transcript (sometimes).

**Up next / recommended sidebar (right side, web)**
- Lists recommended videos. Includes a mix of:
  - More videos from the same channel.
  - Related videos from other channels.
  - Continuation of a playlist (if you opened a playlist).
- **Autoplay toggle** at the top of this panel — when on, YouTube will automatically play
  the next video when the current one ends (a 5-second countdown with a cancel option appears).

## 4. Subtitles and Captions

### What are subtitles / captions?
Captions are text that appear at the bottom of the video, showing what is being said.
YouTube calls them **CC (Closed Captions)** or **Subtitles**. They come in two types:
- **Creator-uploaded captions** — the channel owner manually wrote or uploaded an .srt / .vtt file.
  These are usually the most accurate.
- **Auto-generated captions (ASR)** — YouTube's speech recognition automatically transcribes
  the audio. They are marked with **(auto-generated)** in the language list. Accuracy depends
  on the audio quality and accent; they can contain errors.

---

### Turn subtitles on and off

**On the website:**
- Click the **CC / Subtitles button (▭)** in the bottom-right area of the player controls.
- When subtitles are ON, the button has a solid or underlined appearance (with a red underline).
- When subtitles are OFF, the button looks faint/outline.
- Alternatively, press the **C** key on your keyboard to toggle instantly.

**On the mobile app:**
- Tap the video screen once to reveal the controls.
- Tap the **CC** icon (top-right corner of the video player, looks like a small rectangle
  with lines).
- Or tap the **⋮ (three dots)** → **Captions** to choose the language.
- Or tap the **gear icon (⚙)** that appears in the top controls → **Captions**.

---

### Choose the caption language

1. Click/tap the **gear (⚙)** icon in the player.
2. Select **Subtitles/CC** from the menu.
3. A list of available languages appears. Click/tap your preferred one.
4. If only one language is available (e.g. just English), you can still choose
   **Auto-translate** and then pick any target language (e.g. Turkish, Spanish, French, Arabic).
   YouTube will machine-translate the captions on the fly.
5. The captions update immediately.

---

### Change how subtitles look (appearance)

**On the website:**
1. Click the **gear (⚙)** → **Subtitles/CC** → select a language → then click **Options**.
2. In the Options panel you can change:
   - **Font family** — Basic, Serif, Casual, Cursive, Small Caps, Monospace Serif, Proportional Serif.
   - **Font color** — White, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Magenta, Red, Black.
   - **Font size** — 50%, 75%, 100%, 150%, 200%, 300%, 400%.
   - **Background color** — same color options as font.
   - **Background opacity** — 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%.
   - **Window color** and **Window opacity** — a box around the entire subtitle area.
   - **Character edge style** — None, Drop Shadow, Raised, Depressed, Uniform.
3. Changes apply immediately so you can preview in real time.
4. Click **Reset** to go back to the defaults.

**Account-wide caption settings (web):**
- Go to **avatar → Settings → Playback and performance**.
- Under **Subtitles and captions**, tick **Always show captions** to have them on by default
  for every video.
- Tick **Show automatic captions (when available)** to include auto-generated ones.

**Account-wide caption settings (mobile):**
- Go to your **avatar → Settings → Captions**.
- **Caption language** — set your preferred language so YouTube auto-selects it on every video.
- **Caption size and style** — the same font/color/size options as the web.
- **Always show captions** — toggle to always show captions without having to tap CC each time.

---

### Caption / subtitle troubleshooting

- **No CC button on the player** — the video has no captions at all (the creator hasn't added any
  and auto-captions are not available — this happens for music-heavy videos, very new uploads, or
  non-supported languages).
- **Captions are wrong/inaccurate** — these are auto-generated. Nothing can be done as a viewer
  other than switching to a manually uploaded track if available.
- **Captions appear too fast or slow** — this can happen at non-standard playback speeds. It's a
  known YouTube limitation; adjust playback speed toward 1×.
- **Captions cover the video on mobile** — drag them up or down (on some app versions you can
  tap and drag the caption text to reposition it).

## 5. Downloads (Offline)

### Requirements
Downloading videos for offline playback requires an active **YouTube Premium** subscription.
There is no free way to download videos within the YouTube app. Downloads are stored inside
the app and cannot be exported as regular video files to your device's file system.

---

### Download a video (mobile)

**Method 1 — Download button under the player:**
1. Open the video you want to download.
2. Below the player, tap the **Download** button (a downward arrow ⬇ icon, between Like and Share).
3. A quality picker may appear — choose your preferred quality:
   - **Low (360p)** — uses the least storage (roughly 50–80 MB per hour of video).
   - **Medium (480p)** — balanced quality and size.
   - **High (720p / 1080p)** — best quality but larger file size (500 MB–1 GB per hour).
4. Tap your choice. The download starts in the background.
5. A progress indicator (circular loading ring) appears on the Download button and also
   in the **Library → Downloads** section.

**Method 2 — Three-dot menu:**
1. Tap the **⋮ (three dots / more options)** button next to the video title or on a search result.
2. Tap **Download** from the menu.
3. Choose quality and confirm.

**Method 3 — From the Library:**
1. Go to **You (Library) → Playlists → [open a playlist]**.
2. Tap **Download all** at the top of the playlist to queue all videos for download.

---

### Find and watch downloaded videos

1. Tap **You** (or **Library**) at the bottom of the app.
2. Tap **Downloads**.
3. All downloaded videos are listed here with a green downloaded checkmark.
4. Tap any video to play it. It plays **without using any mobile data** — even in airplane mode.
5. When offline, downloaded videos have a small offline icon to show they are available.

---

### Delete a downloaded video

**Delete one video:**
1. Go to **You / Library → Downloads**.
2. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** next to the video.
3. Tap **Delete download** (or **Remove download**).
4. The video disappears from Downloads but still exists on YouTube — you can re-download it later.

Alternatively:
1. Open the video from Downloads.
2. Tap the **Downloaded ✓ (green checkmark)** button under the player.
3. Tap **Delete** in the confirmation popup.

**Delete all downloads at once:**
1. Go to **Settings → Downloads**.
2. Tap **Delete all downloads**.
3. Confirm. All offline videos are removed and the storage space is freed.

---

### Manage download settings

Go to **Settings → Downloads**:
- **Download quality** — set your default quality so you don't get asked every time
  (Low / Medium / High).
- **Download over Wi-Fi only** — toggle ON to prevent downloads from using mobile data.
  Recommended to avoid large data charges.
- **Storage location** — on some Android devices, choose between **Internal storage**
  and **SD card** if your phone supports expandable storage.
- **Storage used by downloads** — shows how many MB/GB the current downloads are using.
- **Delete all downloads** — see above.

---

### Notes about downloads
- Downloads **expire** — YouTube Premium downloads have a maximum offline period. If you
  don't open the YouTube app and connect to the internet within approximately **30 days**,
  downloads expire and must be re-downloaded. Opening the app while online refreshes the timer.
- Downloads are **device-specific** — you cannot share downloaded videos between devices.
- Downloads are **DRM-protected** — they are encrypted and can only play inside the YouTube app.
  You cannot copy them to another app or computer as a playable file.
- If your **YouTube Premium subscription lapses**, all downloads become unplayable until you
  re-subscribe (they are not deleted, just locked).

## 6. Uploading Videos

### Supported formats and requirements
Before uploading, make sure your file meets YouTube's requirements:
- **Accepted formats:** MP4 (recommended), MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, MKV, MPEG, WebM, 3GP, OGV.
- **Maximum file size:** 256 GB.
- **Maximum video length:** 15 minutes by default. To upload longer videos, verify your account:
  go to **youtube.com/verify** and confirm your phone number — this raises the limit to 12 hours.
- **Resolution:** any resolution up to 8K (7680×4320). YouTube will encode and serve multiple
  quality levels automatically.
- **Frame rate:** 24, 25, 30, 48, 50, or 60 fps recommended.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 is standard. 9:16 vertical is used for Shorts. Other ratios get
  black bars.

---

### Upload a video — website (step by step)

1. Go to **youtube.com** and make sure you are signed in.
2. Click the **Create button (＋ / camera icon)** in the top-right → **Upload video**.
   You are taken to **YouTube Studio** (studio.youtube.com) with the upload dialog open.
3. **Select your file:**
   - Drag your video file from your computer directly into the dotted-border upload area, or
   - Click **Select files** to open a file picker and browse to your video.
   - You can select multiple videos at once to batch-upload them.
4. While the video uploads and processes, you fill in the details on four steps:

---

**Step 1 of 4 — Details:**

- **Title (required):** a clear, descriptive title (max 100 characters). This is the most
  important text for search discoverability. Include keywords naturally.
- **Description:** up to 5,000 characters. Add a detailed description, chapter timestamps
  (format: 0:00 Intro, 1:30 Topic 1, etc.), links, social handles, credits.
- **Thumbnail:** YouTube auto-selects three frames as thumbnail options. To upload a custom one:
  click **Upload thumbnail** and choose an image from your computer. Custom thumbnails must be
  under 2 MB, in JPG/PNG/GIF/BMP format, and ideally 1280×720 pixels (16:9 ratio).
  Custom thumbnails require your account to be verified (youtube.com/verify).
- **Audience — Is this video made for kids?** (required by law, COPPA):
  - **Yes, it's made for kids** — disables comments, personalized ads, and some features.
  - **No, it's not made for kids** — standard upload.
  - If you set it incorrectly, YouTube can penalize your channel.
- **Age restriction** (under "Show more"):
  - Tick **Yes, restrict my video to viewers over 18** if content is mature.
- **Show more** expands additional fields:
  - **Paid promotion** — tick if the video contains sponsored content or paid ads.
  - **Tags** — keywords separated by commas (not as important as title/description for SEO,
    but still useful for disambiguation). Max 500 characters.
  - **Language** — the spoken language of the video.
  - **Caption certification** — for broadcast regulatory compliance (usually not applicable
    for personal channels).
  - **Recording date and location** — optional metadata.
  - **Category** — choose the most fitting category: Film & Animation, Autos & Vehicles,
    Music, Pets & Animals, Sports, Short Movies, Travel & Events, Gaming, Videoblogging,
    People & Blogs, Comedy, Entertainment, News & Politics, Howto & Style, Education,
    Science & Technology, Nonprofits & Activism.
  - **License and distribution:**
    - **Standard YouTube license** — YouTube's default terms; you retain copyright but grant
      YouTube a license to show it.
    - **Creative Commons (CC BY)** — others can reuse your video freely with attribution.
    - **Allow embedding** — tick to let others embed your video on external websites.
    - **Publish to subscriptions feed and notify subscribers** — untick if you don't want to
      notify subscribers (e.g. backdating old content).
  - **Short title** — a shorter title shown in some mobile interfaces.
  - **Content declaration** — declare if the video contains synthetic/AI-generated content
    that is realistic.

---

**Step 2 of 4 — Video elements:**

- **Add subtitles** — upload a caption file (.srt, .vtt, .sbv) or type them manually in the editor.
  You can also use YouTube's automatic transcription as a starting point and edit it.
- **Add an end screen** — appears in the last 5–20 seconds of the video. Add up to 4 elements:
  - **Subscribe button** — prompts viewers to subscribe.
  - **Video or playlist** — links to another video or a playlist on your channel (or the
    "best for viewer" YouTube-chosen recommendation).
  - **Channel** — links to another channel.
  - **Link** — links to an external website (must be in the YouTube Partner Program or an
    approved external site).
  - Drag the elements on the preview timeline to position when they appear.
- **Add cards** — interactive info bubbles that appear mid-video (up to 5 cards):
  - Link to another video, playlist, channel, or external URL.
  - Click the **+** at any point on the timeline to insert a card.

---

**Step 3 of 4 — Checks:**

- YouTube automatically scans your video for **copyright issues** using its Content ID system.
- If a **claim** is found, you'll see a warning: the copyright holder may monetize, mute, or
  block the video. You can dispute or acknowledge the claim.
- **Ad suitability** check — tells you if the content is suitable for all advertisers or only
  limited advertising.
- You can proceed even with unresolved checks; they can be addressed after publishing.

---

**Step 4 of 4 — Visibility:**

- **Public** — anyone on the internet can find and watch it. Shows in search results, feeds,
  and your channel's Videos tab.
- **Unlisted** — only people who have the direct link can watch it. It does NOT appear in search,
  your channel page, or any feed. Great for sharing privately or embedding on your website
  without it being findable.
- **Private** — only you can watch it. You can invite specific Google accounts to view it
  (up to 50 people via their email). They must be signed into YouTube to watch.
- **Schedule** — set a future date and time for the video to automatically switch from Private
  to Public. YouTube shows a countdown timer to creators.

Once you choose visibility, click **Publish** (or **Save** / **Schedule**).
The video begins processing — lower qualities (360p, 480p) are available within minutes;
higher qualities (1080p, 4K) may take 30 minutes to several hours depending on video length.

---

### Upload from the mobile app

1. Tap the **＋ Create** button at the bottom of the app.
2. Tap **Upload a video**.
3. Your device's camera roll / gallery opens. Tap a video to select it.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. **Trim** the video using the handles on the timeline (optional).
6. Add filters, text overlays, or music (if uploading as a Short — see Section 12).
7. For regular videos: add a **Title**, **Description**, and set **Visibility** (Public / Unlisted / Private).
8. Tap **Upload video**. The video uploads in the background.
9. You'll get a notification when the upload and processing are complete.

---

### Check upload progress
- On the web: after clicking Publish, a confirmation screen shows with a link to your video.
  The video processes in your **YouTube Studio → Content** tab — a progress bar shows.
- On mobile: a notification appears when done. You can also check **You → Your videos**.

## 9. Playlists

### What is a playlist?
A playlist is an ordered collection of videos. They play automatically one after another.
You can create playlists to organize videos by topic, share a curated list with others,
or just collect things you want to watch later.

---

### Create a playlist

**From a video:**
1. Click **Save** (bookmark icon) under the video.
2. A panel slides out with a list of your existing playlists. Each has a checkbox.
3. Click **＋ Create new playlist** at the bottom.
4. Type a **name** for the playlist (max 150 characters).
5. Set **visibility:**
   - **Public** — anyone can find and watch it.
   - **Unlisted** — anyone with the link can watch it, but it won't appear in search or on your channel.
   - **Private** — only you can see it.
6. Click **Create**. The video is added immediately.

**From YouTube Studio:**
1. Go to **YouTube Studio → Content → Playlists tab**.
2. Click **New playlist** in the top-right.
3. Give it a name, description, and visibility → **Create**.

---

### Add videos to a playlist

**Method 1 — Save button:**
1. Under any video, click **Save**.
2. Tick the checkbox next to any playlist name. The video is added instantly.

**Method 2 — Three-dot menu:**
- On a video card in a feed, click the **⋮ (three dots)** → **Save to playlist**.

**Method 3 — From the playlist page:**
1. Open the playlist.
2. Click the **＋ Add videos** button.
3. Search for a video by name or paste a URL.

---

### Watch Later
**Watch Later** is a special built-in playlist. It cannot be deleted, made public, or renamed.
- Click **Save → Watch later** under any video.
- Access it at **You / Library → Watch later**.
- On mobile: tap **Save → Watch later**.
- Watch Later videos are private — only you can see them.

---

### Find your playlists
- **Web:** left sidebar → **You / Library → Playlists** or go directly to
  **youtube.com/feed/playlists**.
- **Mobile:** tap **You** → **Playlists**.

### Reorder videos in a playlist
1. Open the playlist.

### Remove a video from a playlist
1. Open the playlist.
2. Click/tap the **⋮ (three dots)** next to the video.

### Delete a playlist

## 13. Live Streaming

### Watch a live stream
- Live streams appear in the **Subscriptions** feed (with a red LIVE badge), **Trending → Live**,
  and on channel pages under the **Live** tab.
- When watching a live stream, the player looks similar to a regular video with these differences:
  - A **red LIVE** badge replaces the timestamp.
  - A **live chat panel** appears on the right (web) or below the video (mobile).
  - The progress bar shows the live edge (current moment). Drag it left to watch from earlier
    in the stream (**DVR** — supported on most streams). Click **LIVE** to jump back to the live edge.
  - **Latency mode** — some streams have low latency mode: chat messages and video are closer
    to real time (~2–5 seconds delay vs ~20–30 seconds for normal latency).

### Live chat
- Type a message in the chat box and press Enter (web) or tap Send (mobile) to chat.
- Only signed-in users can chat.
- **Super Chat** — pay a one-time amount to pin your chat message at the top of the chat
  for a duration. Amounts range from $1 to $500+.
- **Super Stickers** — pay to send an animated sticker that appears in chat.
- **Members-only chat** — some streams require a channel membership to chat.
- Click any username in chat to see their profile or report them.
- Moderators (set by the creator) can slow mode, members-only mode, or timeout/ban users.

### Stream a live stream (go live)
1. Click **Create → Go live** (web) or tap **＋ Create → Go live** (mobile).
2. **Choose stream type:**
   - **Webcam** — stream directly from your browser with no extra software.
   - **Mobile** — stream from the app.
   - **Streaming software (RTMP)** — use OBS, Streamlabs, XSplit, etc. YouTube gives you
     a **Stream key** and **Stream URL** to paste into your software.
3. Add a **title**, **description**, **thumbnail**, **visibility**, **category**, and
   **age restriction**.
4. Schedule: go live now, or set a date/time (creates a waiting room that subscribers can visit).
5. Click **Go live** (webcam) or copy the Stream key and start broadcasting from your software.

**Requirements for mobile live streaming:**
- Your channel must have at least **50 subscribers** to go live on mobile.

## 16. YouTube Premium

### What is YouTube Premium?
YouTube Premium is a paid subscription that upgrades your YouTube experience. It is available at
**youtube.com/premium** or via **avatar → Purchases and memberships**.

---

### What Premium includes

**1. Ad-free videos**
All videos play without any ads — no pre-roll ads, mid-roll ads, banner ads, or sponsored cards.
This applies everywhere: regular videos, Shorts, live streams, and YouTube Music.

**2. Background play**
Audio continues playing even when you:
- Lock your phone screen.
- Switch to another app.
- Turn off the screen completely.
This is the most popular feature for music listeners and podcast watchers.

**3. Offline downloads**
Download videos to watch without an internet connection. See Section 5 for full details.

**4. YouTube Music Premium**
Premium includes a full YouTube Music Premium subscription — ad-free music streaming,
background play for music, offline downloads of songs and albums, and access to the full
YouTube Music library (songs, albums, playlists, remixes, and live versions).

**5. 1080p Enhanced (Premium quality)**
On supported videos, Premium subscribers get access to a higher bitrate 1080p encoding —
the same resolution but more visual detail and less compression artefact.

**6. YouTube Originals**
Access to YouTube's original shows, movies, and documentaries (though many have been
made free; the catalogue varies by region).

---

### How to subscribe to YouTube Premium
1. Go to **youtube.com/premium** or click **avatar → Purchases and memberships**.
2. Click **Get YouTube Premium**.
3. Choose a plan:
   - **Individual** — one account.
   - **Family** — up to 5 family members in the same household for a higher monthly fee.
   - **Student** — discounted plan for verified university/college students.
4. Enter your payment method (credit/debit card, PayPal, or carrier billing on mobile).
5. Click **Buy** / **Subscribe**.
6. Premium activates immediately — ads disappear right away.

---

### Manage or cancel YouTube Premium
1. Go to **youtube.com/paid_memberships** or **avatar → Purchases and memberships → YouTube Premium**.
2. Click **Manage membership**.
3. To cancel: click **Deactivate** or **Cancel membership**.
4. Premium features continue until the end of your billing period.
5. After cancellation, ads return, background play stops, and downloads become unplayable
   (but are not deleted until you choose to remove them).

---

### YouTube Premium on TV and Chromecast
- Sign into the YouTube app on your TV with your Premium account.
- Ads are removed on TVs, gaming consoles (PlayStation, Xbox), and Chromecast devices
  as long as you're signed in with your Premium account.
- Background play on TV means audio continues when you switch TV inputs (depends on TV OS).

## 18. Troubleshooting Common Problems

### Video won't play / stuck buffering

**On web:**
- Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R).
- Clear your browser cache: browser menu → Settings → Clear browsing data → tick Cached images
  and files → Clear.
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
- Disable browser extensions — particularly ad blockers, VPNs, or privacy extensions —
  that can interfere with YouTube's video player. Try in an incognito/private window.
- Lower the video quality: gear (⚙) → Quality → choose 360p or 480p to reduce buffering.
- Check your internet speed at fast.com. YouTube needs at least:
  - 3 Mbps for 1080p, 25 Mbps for 4K.
- If using a VPN, try disabling it — some VPN exit nodes are throttled for video.

**On mobile:**
- Close and reopen the YouTube app.
- Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection (open another app to confirm the internet works).
- Force-stop the YouTube app: Settings → Apps → YouTube → Force stop.
- Clear the app cache: Settings → Apps → YouTube → Storage → Clear Cache
  (this does not delete your data or downloaded videos).
- Update the app in the Play Store or App Store.
- Restart your phone.

---

### Can't sign in

- Make sure you're entering the correct email address — check for typos (e.g. @gmail.com vs @googlemail.com).
- Click **Forgot password** and reset it via your recovery email or phone.
- If you get "This account doesn't exist", your account may have been deleted or you may be
  using the wrong email.
- If you set up 2-Step Verification and can't access your phone or authenticator, use a
  **backup code** (saved when you set up 2FA) or contact Google Account recovery at
  accounts.google.com/signin/recovery.
- Try clearing browser cookies for google.com and youtube.com and signing in again.

---

### YouTube app crashing

- Force-stop the app and reopen it.
- Clear the app cache (not data — see above).
- Uninstall and reinstall the YouTube app.
- Make sure your Android/iOS is updated to a supported version.
- Check if there's a known YouTube outage at downdetector.com/status/youtube.

---

### Subtitles not showing

- Press **C** (web) or tap the CC button (mobile) to ensure captions are on.
- If the CC button is missing entirely, this video has no captions available.
- Make sure you haven't accidentally set the caption language to a language that doesn't
  exist for this video. Go to gear (⚙) → Subtitles/CC → choose an available language.

---

### Video quality is bad / blurry

- On web: gear (⚙) → Quality → choose a higher value manually (720p, 1080p).
- The quality may be auto-set low because YouTube detected a slow connection. Try setting it
  to a specific higher quality.
- If the video only tops out at 360p, the uploader only uploaded it in 360p — there's no
  higher quality available for that video.

---

### Can't upload a video

- Make sure the file format is supported (see Section 6).
- If your video is longer than 15 minutes, verify your account at youtube.com/verify.
- Check that your file isn't corrupted — try playing it in a local media player first.
- Check available storage on your device.
- Try a different browser on the web.
- If you get a copyright claim during upload, it doesn't block the upload — you can still
  publish (the claimant may monetize it, but you can dispute).

---

### Comments aren't loading

- Refresh the page.
- Scroll down fully — YouTube loads comments lazily after the video loads.
- Some videos have comments disabled by the creator.
- If comments are disabled, you'll see a message: "Comments are turned off" or a lock icon
  with "Comments are limited" (meaning the creator restricted who can comment).

---

### Download not working / greyed out

- Downloads require YouTube Premium. If you don't have it, the download button is greyed out
  or hidden. Subscribe at youtube.com/premium.
- If you have Premium and the button is still greyed out, the creator has disabled downloads
  for their video.
- Make sure the YouTube app is updated to the latest version.

---

### Can't find a video I watched before

- Check your **History** (Section 10). Search by keyword in the history search bar.
- If you cleared your history or had history paused, the video may not be there.
- The video may have been deleted by the creator or removed by YouTube.
- Try searching for the video title or topic on YouTube to find it again.

---

### YouTube is showing videos in the wrong language

- Click your **avatar** → **Language** → select your language.
- Also check **Location** in the avatar menu — this affects regional content.
- On mobile: avatar → Settings → General → Language.

---

### Ads are still showing with YouTube Premium

- Make sure you're signed into the account that has Premium (check the avatar).
- If using a browser extension that might inject content, it may conflict with Premium's
  ad removal. Try disabling extensions.
- On a shared TV: make sure the TV's YouTube app is signed into your Premium account.

---

### Video shows "This video isn't available in your country"

- The creator or rights holder has geo-restricted the video.
- A VPN set to a country where the video is available may allow viewing — but this is
  against YouTube's Terms of Service.
- There may be an officially licensed version of the content in your country on a different
  channel or platform.
